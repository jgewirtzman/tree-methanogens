## Draft Methods subsection (drop into main-text Methods)

> **### 2.X Whole-tree profiling of a felled black oak (*Quercus velutina*)**
>
> To resolve vertical and tissue-level patterns of methane, internal gas
> concentrations, and methane-cycling gene abundance within a single tree, we
> intensively sampled one mature black oak (*Quercus velutina*) in a mixed
> deciduous stand at Yale-Myers Forest — the same individual described in Arnold
> & Gewirtzman et al. (2025), here augmented with new flux, gas, and gene-abundance
> measurements. The tree had a visible basal seam (lower ~1.5 m) indicating heart
> rot, later confirmed, but was otherwise healthy with a full canopy and minimal
> external wounding. Stem CH₄ flux and internal gas concentrations were measured
> at seven heights (0.5, 1.25, 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10 m) on the standing tree
> (heights >2 m accessed via arborist climbing equipment), following the chamber
> flux and increment-borer gas-sampling protocols described in Sections [X, Y].
> The tree was then felled with a chainsaw (4 October 2022, before leaf
> senescence) to permit destructive sampling not possible on a standing tree.
> Immediately after felling, increment cores were collected at each of the seven
> heights and split into heartwood (inner 5 cm) and sapwood (outer 5 cm), and
> additional samples of leaves, bark, branches, leaf litter, heart rot, and
> coarse (>2 mm) and fine (≤2 mm) roots were collected, together with soil,
> following Arnold & Gewirtzman et al. (2025); to minimize contamination,
> tissue samples were taken only from surfaces not in contact with the ground.
> All samples were frozen on dry ice and stored at −80 °C, then lyophilized,
> cryo-ground, and processed for DNA extraction and ddPCR quantification of
> mcrA, pmoA, and mmoX as for the main survey (Section [X]); root, leaf, bark,
> litter, and heart-rot extracts were additionally treated with a PCR-inhibitor
> removal kit. Flux, internal-gas, and gene-abundance data for this tree are
> shown in Fig. 7 and Fig. S12; this individual was analyzed separately and is
> not included in the cross-species or upscaling analyses.
