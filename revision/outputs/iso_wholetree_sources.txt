RECOMMENDATION (CORRECTED): do NOT use Miller-Tans here. Despite its high R2,
Miller-Tans is dominated by a few high-CH4 points -- its CH4 source swings
-65 -> -89 permil as the top 1-5 points are dropped (the R2 is a concentration-
weighting artifact, not fit quality). KEELING is the robust estimate here
(stable ~ -79 permil to point removal). Better still, report the bulk median
d13CH4 (-63.7, manuscript) plus the atmosphere-mass-balance tree-produced
source (~ -70 permil, endmember-robust; see R_isotopes_convergence_threshold.R).
The tree-produced source is MORE depleted than the measured bulk because the
measured gas is diluted with atmospheric CH4 -- consistent with hydrogenotrophy.
