=================================================================
WITHIN-TREE CH4-CO2 APPARENT FRACTIONATION (the appropriate unit)
=================================================================

Each whole-tree sample has paired d13CH4 + d13CO2, so eps_C is computed
WITHIN each tree, then summarized across trees. This is the appropriate
way to look at CH4-vs-CO2 fractionation.

CONSISTENT paired medians (d13CH4, d13CO2, eps_C from the SAME samples):
 ch4_threshold   n d13CH4 d13CO2 eps_from_medians
           1.5 130  -63.0  -20.4             45.4
          10.0  59  -69.2  -19.3             53.5
          50.0  37  -70.5  -17.6             56.9
         100.0  31  -69.7  -17.3             56.3

KEY: d13CH4 (a COMPOSITION) and eps_C (a DIFFERENCE) are different quantities.
 * Bulk (all samples): d13CH4 ~ -62 (manuscript -63.7), d13CO2 ~ -21, eps_C ~ 44.
 * Reliable (>=50 ppm): d13CH4 ~ -70 (more depleted), d13CO2 ~ -18, eps_C ~ 55.
 -> the -63 d13CH4 pairs with eps_C ~44, NOT ~60. eps_C ~55 goes with the more-
    depleted high-CH4 d13CH4 (~-70). Always report both from the SAME subset.

CONTRAST — the pooled-source approach CONFLATES pools:
  Miller-Tans CH4 source=-65, CO2=-35 -> paired eps_C=32.
  This eps_C (~32) is WRONG: the CO2 source (-35) is weighted toward high-CO2
  trees, but the CO2 that co-occurs with high CH4 is ~-18. Within-tree eps_C
  (~55) pairs each tree's own CH4 and CO2 and is the valid estimate.
  (NB: standards like S3c, d13CH4 -391 at 16105 ppm, must be excluded or they
   hijack the Miller-Tans slope -- that earlier gave a spurious -269.)

HOW TO REPORT:
 * Primary isotopic evidence = depleted d13CH4 (-63.7, manuscript) + taxonomy.
 * Also report d13CO2 (~-20) and the apparent eps_C from the SAME samples:
   bulk eps_C ~44, rising to ~55 (boundary of the CO2-reduction range) as CH4
   increases and the signal cleans up. eps_C is CORROBORATING, not decisive.
 * Caveat: eps_C uses bulk (respiration) CO2, not the isolated methanogenic
   substrate, so it likely mis-estimates the true fractionation (R2 L607-610).
