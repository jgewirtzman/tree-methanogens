CAVEATS (report prominently): (1) gene copies are NOT activity or flux;
(2) soil methanogen CH4 is largely oxidized before emission (soils net sinks
here) whereas wood methanogens sit near the emitting bark surface, so equal
POOLS do NOT imply equal emissions; (3) copies/g are extraction lower bounds;
(4) soil dry masses use black-oak GWC + literature bulk densities; (5) shallow
soil only (~20 cm) -- deeper soil adds mass but little methanogen relevance here.
This is a bounding CONTEXT scenario, not a budget.
