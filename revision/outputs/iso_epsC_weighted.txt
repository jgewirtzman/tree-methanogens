Why NOT naive inverse-variance weighting: it assumes trees are identical
(tau=0), so the single highest-CH4 point dominates and SE collapses to ~0 --
the Miller-Tans leverage trap. The random-effects model estimates real
between-tree spread (tau ~ tens of permil) and so weights sensibly.
