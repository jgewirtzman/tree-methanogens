## Results — isotopes

Whole-tree samples: n=130 with d13C-CH4 (n=130 also with paired d13C-CO2).

### Composition
- d13C-CH4 (bulk median) = -63.0 permil (IQR -72.2 to -46.5)
- d13C-CO2 (bulk median) = -20.4 permil (IQR -22 to -17.2)

### CH4 source signature
- Keeling intercept = -79 permil [95% CI -92, -66]; robust (drop top 0/3/5 high-CH4 pts: -79/-80/-80)
- Atmosphere mixing-corrected = -70 permil (endmember-robust: -70/-71/-71 for d_atm -47/-40/-35)
- Miller-Tans = -65 permil; its slope is carried by the few highest-CH4 points
  (drop top 0/3/5 -> -65/-80/-89), so it is determined by, not robust to, those points.
  Keeling and Miller-Tans weight opposite ends of the concentration range; we take
  the atmosphere correction (-70) as the independent tie-breaker between them.
- Interpretation: tree-produced source (~-70 to -79 permil) is more depleted than the
  bulk measured value because measured gas is diluted with atmospheric CH4.

### Apparent CO2-CH4 fractionation (eps_C)
- bulk within-tree median eps_C = 47 permil
- from source-corrected CH4: eps_C = 54 (atmosphere-corrected) to 64 (Keeling) permil
  [= f(source d13CH4, median d13CO2 -20); this ASSUMES methanogenic CO2 ~ bulk CO2]
- CO2-CH4 coupling: r=-0.03 overall (p=0.77); r=0.28 at >=10 ppm (p=0.032) -> WEAK.
  Bulk CO2 is largely respiration-dominated, so the methanogenic-CO2 ~ bulk-CO2 assumption
  is questionable and eps_C is an apparent (corroborating) indicator with unknown-direction
  bias, NOT a measured fractionation.
- Among-species d13C-CH4: no detectable difference at reliable CH4 (Kruskal p=0.44), but
  only 6 species-groups / 45 samples entered the test (of 57/59 matched to species),
  i.e. modest power -> consistent with, not proof of, a single pooled source.

### Bottom line
Depleted d13C-CH4 (bulk -63.0; tree-produced ~-70 to -79 permil), a within-tree
apparent CO2-CH4 fractionation of ~54-64 permil (at/into the CO2-reduction range),
and hydrogenotrophic Methanobacteriaceae dominance are together consistent with
substantial hydrogenotrophic methanogenesis. The taxonomy is the primary evidence;
eps_C is consistent-with, not diagnostic (respiration-dominated bulk CO2, d13C only,
and possible oxidation shifting the apparent source).
