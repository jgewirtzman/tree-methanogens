WHAT'S CORRECT / RECOMMENDATION:
  * All three are internally correct; they answer different questions.
  * Keep the manuscript's Method-2 numbers (5.3% species-unique, 82.9%
    unexplained) as the published partition, but call it what it is: an
    OLS hierarchical partition, NOT a mixed model. Caveat: the interaction
    R2 is inflated by many species x env parameters, so 'unexplained=82.9%'
    is a slight lower bound on the true residual.
  * For the aggregation argument, the ICC is the cleaner statistic: only
    ~7% (raw) / ~16% (arcsinh) of INDIVIDUAL flux variance is between
    species; the large within-species remainder is exactly the point-
    sampling heterogeneity that motivates species-level aggregation.
