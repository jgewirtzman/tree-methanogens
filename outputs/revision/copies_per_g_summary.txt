DRY/WET STANDARDIZATION (answer to R2):
  * To a COMMON basis we can convert WOOD dry->fresh using core moisture
    (fresh & dry masses per core), giving everything on a FRESH/wet basis
    (soil already fresh). This is the feasible common unit without soil GWC.
  * All-DRY is only possible where soil GWC exists (black-oak soils have GWC;
    main-study soils do not).
  * Per-SAMPLE wood moisture join uses tree_id_comprehensive_mapping.csv
    (data/processed/tree_data/) to bridge ddPCR ids <-> tree_id.
  * Soil: gravimetric soil moisture per ddPCR soil sample is NOT in the
    compiled ddPCR table; VWC (volumetric) exists per tree/plot but is not a
    direct gravimetric conversion. To standardize soil to dry weight we need
    the soil sample gravimetric moisture (or to state soil is fresh-basis).
  * Recommendation: report ALL copies/g on a DRY-weight basis; convert wood
    with tissue moisture; for soil either supply gravimetric moisture or
    state the basis explicitly and note it does not affect wood>>soil.
