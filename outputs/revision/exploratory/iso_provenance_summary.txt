Sample classes (ch4>=1.5 ppm):
      class   n
 Atmosphere   3

(A) WHOLE-TREE single-borehole = the manuscript basis (11a):
    n=130; median d13CH4 = -63.0 permil (manuscript reports -63.7)
    median d13CO2 = -20.4; median eps_C = 47 permil

(B) PAIRED HEARTWOOD vs SAPWOOD (tissue-resolved 'second project'):
    11 trees with both H and S.
    median d13CH4  Heartwood = -68.8 ; Sapwood = -70.4 permil
    median SW-HW CH4 enrichment = -0.7 permil  (paired Wilcoxon p=0.206)
    median SW-HW CO2 enrichment = -0.7 permil

INTERPRETATION (honest): oxidation of CH4 diffusing from heartwood out through
oxic sapwood would predict sapwood CH4 to be MORE 13C-enriched (positive SW-HW).
We do NOT see that: SW-HW is small and slightly NEGATIVE (-0.7 permil) and NOT
significant (p=0.21, n=11 paired). So the tissue-resolved isotopes do NOT provide
clear evidence of sapwood oxidation enrichment. The oxidation story should rest
on the gene distribution (methanotrophs enriched in sapwood; pmoA:mmoX), not on
HW/SW isotopic enrichment. Caveats: small n after QC, noisy raw deltas at low CH4;
the incubations (V_*) may be a better-controlled oxidation test if designed for it.
(Also confirm suffix H=heartwood, S=sapwood before using at all.)

WHICH DATASET FOR WHAT:

  * Heartwood vs sapwood enrichment     -> PAIRED H/S set (this script).
  * Incubations (V_*, Run 4)            -> separate resource, not in-situ.
